# Attempts

Every attempt runs its step's prompt from `steps/`, unchanged, on its own branch
`step-NN-<name>/<person>/attempt-N`, with the process installed at the fork commit named below.
Its transcript goes to `attempts/step-NN/<person>/attempt-N.md` on that branch — `/export` in the
terminal version of Claude Code, or a copy of the session's `.jsonl` from `~/.claude/projects/`.
Issues found become commits in the fork.
